# Q911 literature assessment

Checked 2026-11-27. Confidence is high in the source match and attribution of the earlier census. Confidence is moderate in the bounded finding that no checked source publishes a sink-specific witness.

- **Original conjecture.** Restadh’s *Diameters of the Characteristic Imset Polytopes*, arXiv:2302.03647v2, March 6, 2023, defines the sink operation just before Lemma 2.18. Conjecture 1 asks for an edge of the fixed-skeleton face whenever the move changes Markov equivalence class. Theorem 1.4 identifies Markov equivalence with equality of characteristic imsets. The conjecture would imply diameter at most $n-1$ for each fixed skeleton. This witness rules out the specific move-as-edge assertion. The diameter bound itself is a separate question. The KTH dissertation lists the original preprint as Paper III. No later complete edition was identified by title, author and publication-list searches. [Conjecture 1 and Lemma 2.18](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2302.03647v2#page=11) · [KTH dissertation](https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2%3A1757765/FULLTEXT01.pdf)
- **Earlier classification.** Linusson and Restadh, *Rhombus Criterion and the Chordal Graph Polytope*, arXiv:2305.05275, §2, gives the midpoint criterion. Section 3 states the fixed-skeleton face property and computational completeness of the criterion through order five. Section 5 and Table 1 report the full edge structure for the 8782-vertex, 26-dimensional $\operatorname{CIM}_5$. The earlier computation therefore includes this pair, as the fixed-skeleton polytope is a face of the full polytope. The paper does not flag the pair as a sink rewrite. The cited computation repository contains scripts and a placeholder in polytope-data. Its recorded public tree contains no full edge list. [Criterion](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.05275#S2) · [Census and Table 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.05275#S5) · [Code repository](https://github.com/PetterRestadh/rhombus-criterion/tree/e9b46f9dfc28810f559afc875f78714afc11a329)
- **Related adjacency theorems.** Restadh’s Theorem 2.1 quotes adjacency for a single reversible arc whose reversal is acyclic and changes the imset. Here the sink rewrite reverses two arcs at once. The 2026 journal *On the edges of characteristic imset polytopes* describes the edge graph for tree skeletons. The author preprint gives cycle results as well (§3.2), and the witness skeleton is a diamond with a leaf. [Single-arc theorem](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2302.03647v2#page=3) · [Journal edition](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijar.2025.109606) · [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2209.07579#S3)
- **Later neighboring work.** Coons, Hollering and Chakrabarty Paul, *Polyhedral Geometry of Degree-Bounded Characteristic Imset Polytopes*, arXiv:2611.09633v2, November 25, 2026, studies degree-bounded polytopes, with facet results and a complete four-node description for indegree at most two. Its sink constructions concern facet extensions or specially built DAGs. This does not classify the general sink rewrite. [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.09633v2)

The bounded search covered the exact Conjecture 1, later titles and papers by the authors, the KTH publication records, the rhombus enumeration and repository tree, and variants of “characteristic imset”, “sink operation”, “counterexample”, “diamond” and “nonedge”. The explicit witness, lift, and direct verification of small-order minimality are the package's contributions. The literature check establishes no absolute priority.
