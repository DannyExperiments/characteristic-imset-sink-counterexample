# Mathematical review

The sink rewrite reverses exactly 2→0 and 2→1. Four distinct binary imsets share the pair coordinates and the triples 012 and 013. The remaining coordinates split as two equal bits for 123 and 0123 and one independent bit for 024. Opposite corners have equal sum. This suffices for nonadjacency in the whole fixed-skeleton polytope, without a complete classification of its other edges.

With all new vertices after the original five, old vertices cannot supply coordinates containing a new vertex. The new parent sets are the same for all four orientations. The no-new-neighbor condition at vertex 2 preserves the move. The exact minimality replay separately confirms that every smaller changed sink pair is an exposed edge.
